The challenge

Most municipalities don't know where their financial systems actually stand.

Compliance blind spots

State reporting requirements change annually. Without a systematic review, gaps accumulate silently until audit season arrives.

Payroll complexity grows

Special pay types, deferred comp, multi-jurisdiction taxes — legacy systems struggle as your payroll structure evolves beyond their design.

No objective baseline

When vendors pitch new systems, you have no independent assessment to compare against. Every proposal looks compelling without context.
