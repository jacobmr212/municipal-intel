Overview

What is the Municipal Intel Scanner?

An automated lead generation platform that continuously monitors public municipal documents — meeting minutes, procurement pages, budget postings — to surface ERP buying signals before they become RFPs.

Nationwide Coverage

Tracks 1,376+ municipal sources across all 50 states plus DC and Puerto Rico. From meeting minutes to procurement portals to state aggregators.

Targeted Filtering

Filter by state, population tier (2.5K–250K+), and source type. Focus on your ideal market segment.

Signal Detection

AI-powered analysis detects procurement signals, budget discussions, vendor mentions, and system pain points across documents.

Automated Scanning

Set it and forget it. Scans run in the background, analyzing hundreds of documents in 25 minutes on average.

Lead Scoring

Each lead is scored (0–100) based on signal strength, urgency keywords, and procurement stage. Prioritize HOT leads first.

Customer Classification

Leads are automatically tagged as "existing customer" (retention/upsell) or "new opportunity" (acquisition).

Detection Engine

What signals does it detect?

The scanner looks for 138+ weighted keywords across six categories. Each signal type contributes to the overall lead score.

High Priority

Active RFPs

Request for proposal, invitation to bid, solicitation for ERP/finance/payroll systems

High Priority

Budget Signals

Capital improvement plans, IT modernization budgets, ERP line items, software procurement funds

Medium Priority

Direct Mentions

Caselle, Clarity, Tyler, Munis, other ERP vendors mentioned in documents

Medium Priority

System Complaints

Manual processes, reporting issues, compliance gaps, integration problems, training needs

Low Priority

Technical Gaps

Discussions of GL structure, payroll complexity, fund accounting, audit findings

Low Priority

Modernization Intent

Digital transformation, cloud migration, process automation, system consolidation
